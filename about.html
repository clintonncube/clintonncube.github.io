About Me

Get to know more about who I am, my journey, my interests, my goals and my vision for the future.

Who I Am

My name is Thabang Clinton Ncube. I am a student at Silveira High School. I was born on 21 September 2008. I consider myself to be patient, reasonable and cautious. I believe in learning from experience, facing challenges and becoming a better and more mature person every day.

My Story

My journey has been shaped by my experiences in school, sports, technology and everyday life. I developed a strong interest in basketball and computers because they give me ways to focus, learn and relieve stress. Along the way, I have learned to be more careful about the decisions I make and to become more responsible and mature.

My Interests

My main interests include playing basketball, swimming and reading. Basketball is especially important to me because it is not only something I enjoy, but also a possible backup career. These activities give me time to relax, improve myself and relieve stress.

My Skills

I have developed skills in website creation, refereeing basketball and coaching basketball. I am also interested in learning how to become more mature and how to care for other people. I believe that continuously developing my skills will help me prepare for my future.

My School Life

I am a Form 4 student at Silveira High School in 2026. Some of the subjects I enjoy include Mathematics, Physical Education and Pure Mathematics. I enjoy helping others and participating in sports occasions and other school activities.

My Personality

I would describe myself as a person who tries to remain calm, reasonable and cautious. When I face a difficult challenge, I believe in facing it rather than running away from it. I value consistency and prayer, and I believe that both can help me remain focused when working toward my goals.

My Favourite Things

I enjoy snacks, basketball and mathematics. Basketball is one of my favourite activities, and I also follow the San Antonio Spurs. I enjoy playing basketball because it is exciting, challenging and gives me an opportunity to improve my skills.

My Goals

One of my main goals is to become a doctor. I want to continue learning new skills, develop myself academically and eventually work in medicine to help sick people. At the same time, I want to continue developing my basketball abilities and keep the possibility of becoming a professional basketball player open.

My Future Vision

In the future, I see myself working in medicine as a doctor while continuing to develop my basketball career. My strongest ambition is to study medicine and become a doctor who helps people in need. Basketball will always remain an important part of my life and may become another career path for me. I am working toward my future by studying, developing my skills, staying consistent and learning from every experience.
